### page 10 —

* “to qualify as a hack, the feat must be imbued with innovation, style, and technical virtuosity.”

### vocabulary

* **cruft:**  useless or messy code or objects.
* **virtuosity:** exceptional skill or mastery.
* **self-deprecatingly:** in a way that minimizes one's own abilities or achievements.

### page 11 —

* “this man actually thought that computers could be smart.”

### vocabulary

* **absent-minded:** distracted or not paying attention.
* **robotically:** in a mechanical or unnatural way.
* **arrogance:** excessive confidence or a sense of superiority.

### page 12 —

* “the fantastic moment when you got your printout back from the priesthood—word from the source itself!”

### vocabulary

* **boneheaded:** foolish or unintelligent.
* **mammoth:** extremely large or enormous.
* **priesthood:** a group of priests; here, used ironically for technical staff with exclusive access.

### page 13 —
